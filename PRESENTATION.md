## 🛑 Slide 2: The Problem (The Vernacular Civic Disconnect)
* **The Reality:** Over 70% of rural and semi-urban citizens cannot engage with existing government grievance portals.
* **3 Major Bottlenecks:**
  1. **Formal Literacy & Language Barrier:** Portals demand formal English/Hindi typing. Local dialects, idioms, and rural audio cannot be typed or categorized.
  2. **Isolated Ticket Fatigue:** 1,200 villagers complaining about contaminated water or flooded culverts are treated as 1,200 isolated tickets, overwhelming ward officers and burying genuine crises.
  3. **Opaque Allocation Gap:** District budgets are often allocated to who shouts loudest or where political influence sits, rather than data-verified demand clusters with explainable public basis.

---

## 💡 Slide 3: The Solution (Dialect-to-Decision Pipeline)
* **1. Language-First Acoustic Ingestion:**
  Citizens speak or send audio via WhatsApp, Voice Notes, or SMS. Automated local scrubbing strips PII (Aadhaar, phone numbers) while preserving the original spoken dialect quote permanently for auditing.
* **2. Spatial & Semantic Clustering (Google Gemini 2.5 Flash):**
  Instead of isolated complaints, Gemini groups inputs by GPS proximity and semantic taxonomy into high-priority **Civic Demand Hotspots**.
* **3. Explainable Proposals with Human-in-the-Loop Sign-Off:**
  AI calculates engineering budget estimates and ranks projects. District officials verify and approve with 1 click before any public tender is issued.

## 📐 Slide 6: Explainable Prioritization Formula
$$\text{Score} = (0.35 \times \text{Demand}) + (0.25 \times \text{Diversity}) + (0.25 \times \text{Urgency}) + (0.15 \times \text{Feasibility})$$

* **Demand Concentration (35%):** Density of citizen signals per square kilometer.
* **Source Diversity (25%):** Corroboration across independent channels (Voice Notes, WhatsApp, community surveys).
* **Urgency Pattern (25%):** Keyword detection for immediate threats to health, safety, or education.
* **Feasibility & Scheme Fit (15%):** Direct alignment with active municipal scheme budget lines.
