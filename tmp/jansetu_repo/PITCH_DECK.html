The Governance Bottleneck

The Vernacular Civic Disconnect in India

Over 70% of rural and semi-urban citizens cannot engage with existing government grievance portals. Here is why:

📝
Formal Literacy Barrier
Traditional portals require structured text in standard English or Hindi. Vernacular speech, local idioms, and rural audio cannot be typed or categorized.
🗂️
Isolated Ticket Fatigue
When 1,200 villagers complain about water or broken culverts, they are treated as 1,200 isolated tickets, overwhelming ward officers and burying genuine crises.
📉
Opaque Allocation Gap
District budgets are often allocated to who shouts loudest or where political influence sits, rather than data-verified demand clusters with explainable public basis.

The Core Solution

Civics Plus: Dialect-to-Decision Pipeline

An end-to-end civic intelligence pipeline that bridges marginalized citizens directly with public infrastructure planners.

1. Ingest Voice & Text
Acoustic Model (16kHz)
Citizens speak or send audio via WhatsApp, SMS, or web portal. PII (names, phones) is stripped automatically. Original dialect quote is preserved forever.
2. Gemini Spatial Synthesis
Semantic Hotspots
Google Gemini 2.5 Flash extracts needs, checks GPS proximity, and clusters hundreds of reports into actionable hotspots (e.g. Kalyanpur Water Deficit).
3. Explainable Proposals
Human-in-the-Loop Sign-Off
AI drafts procurement briefs and estimates budgets. District Magistrate verifies and approves with 1 click. Zero rogue spending.

Algorithm & Transparency

Explainable Prioritization Formula

No black boxes. Every public rupee recommendation is mathematically justified.

Score = (0.35 × Demand) + (0.25 × Diversity) + (0.25 × Urgency) + (0.15 × Feasibility)
Demand (35%)

Concentration of signals per sq km.

Diversity (25%)

Multiple distinct channels (audio, WhatsApp, surveys).

Urgency (25%)

Keywords of safety, illness, school disruption.

Feasibility (15%)

Alignment with existing departmental budget heads.
